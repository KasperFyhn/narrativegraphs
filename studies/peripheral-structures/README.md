# Peripheral structures - Molecules of a Story

This subfolder holds the analysis code for a conference talk at DHNB 2026 in Aarhus, Denmark and a forthcoming conference paper.

> Fyhn, Kasper & Rebekah Baglini. Forthcoming. Molecules of a Story: Community Detection in PMI-weighted Narrative Networks. In _Proceedings of the 9th Digital Humanities in the Nordic and Baltic Countries Conference (DHNB 2026)_.

The study explores an approach to reveal peripheral narrative structures in narrative networks which generally favor salient structures. The approach, in its simplicity, is to weight edges by PMI which favors rare entities and extract communities. The extracted communities naturally form many smaller structures with rare entities rather than big communities around frequent and central entities.

The version of `narrativegraphs` used to generate results is `v0.4.0`.
